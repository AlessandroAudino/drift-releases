What's new

Every version of Drift, newest first. Drift updates itself, so you always have the latest.

Version 2.5.2

Version 2.5.2

November 26, 2026
  • Christmas Eve and Christmas Day: storybook snow and a Christmas wallpaper, Santa's journey around the world, and his visit at sunset with presents under your tree: a handwritten letter, a Christmas app icon to keep, and a wish. The decorations stay up until the Epiphany.
  • The shortest day of the year, as each place keeps it: lanterns for the longest night, pomegranates for Yalda, a bowl of tangyuan for Dongzhi, a yuzu bath for Tōji, patjuk for Dongji, and sunflowers for the longest day south of the equator.
  • New Year's Eve: a countdown (in the menu bar on a Mac), fireworks at midnight, a box of sparklers to write with light, gold and silver confetti in the snow, the new year arriving city by city around the world, and the first sunrise of the year.
  • The Epiphany, each country its own: la Befana flies in on her broom, sweeps the snow off a window and leaves a stocking of sweets (and a lump of sugar coal), then takes the decorations away at sunset; the Three Kings ride along your screen following the star, leave presents by your shoes and bring a roscón with the little king hidden inside. And Christmas on 7 January, for the countries that keep it then: candles on your windows, the first star on Christmas Eve, presents on Christmas morning.
  • The snowy cabin takes its Christmas decorations down after the Epiphany (or as soon as la Befana has swept them away) and puts them up again on 1 December: new plain winter pictures for every time of day.
  • Write a wish for the new year: Drift keeps it on your computer and gives it back to you on New Year's Eve, a year later. And on 31 December, "Your year in Drift".
  • Festivities ask first: Christmas, New Year's Eve and the Epiphany bring snow and their own wallpaper once you've said yes to their postcard, and a weather or wallpaper you pick yourself stays until they end. You can switch this off in Settings › Festivities.
Version 2.4.3

Version 2.4.3

November 24, 2026
  • Small improvements behind the scenes: a personal invite code can now open more than test versions. Nothing changes if you don't have one.
Version 2.4.1

Version 2.4.1

November 23, 2026
  • Festivities: Drift now celebrates your country's festivities, and invites you to the world's with a postcard that drifts onto your screen. Settings › Festivities, with a countdown to the next one.
  • The Advent calendar, from 1 December: every day Santa brings a numbered box, and each one you open decorates your desktop a little more, until Christmas Eve.
  • Hanukkah, from 4 December: one more light on your windows every evening at sunset, for eight nights.
Version 2.3.0

Version 2.3.0

November 22, 2026
  • The wallpapers follow the real sun: dawn, golden hour and night now arrive at the real sunrise and sunset where you live, all year round.
  • Your place: Drift guesses it from your computer's time zone, and you can pick any city in Settings › Wallpaper. It stays on your computer.
  • Beta versions are now by invitation, with a personal invite code.
Version 2.2.1

Version 2.2.1

November 20, 2026
  • Your first automatic update: from now on, new versions of Drift arrive just like this one.
  • New "Check for updates…" in the menu bar / tray menu.
Version 2.2.0

Version 2.2.0

November 20, 2026
  • Drift now keeps itself up to date: new versions download quietly in the background and install when Drift restarts.
  • A new Updates section in Settings › General shows what's new, lets you check for updates, and has a switch for beta versions.
  • Every download is checked against its fingerprint before it is installed, so a damaged download is never used.
