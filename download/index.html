Which Mac do I have?

Click the Apple menu in the top-left corner and choose About This Mac. If it says Chip: Apple M1, M2, M3 or later, choose Apple silicon. If it says Processor followed by Intel, choose Intel.

How to install

On a Mac

On a Mac

  1. Open the file you downloaded and drag Drift onto the Applications folder.
  2. Open Drift from Applications. It lives in the menu bar at the top of the screen: click its icon to pick a weather or open Settings.

If your Mac stops Drift the first time

Early-access versions aren't registered with Apple yet, so macOS asks before opening Drift the first time. What to do depends on the message:

“Apple could not verify “Drift” is free of malware” or “Drift can't be opened”

Click Done. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security and scroll down. Next to “Drift” was blocked, click Open Anyway, enter your password, then click Open.

“Drift is damaged and can't be opened”

The app is fine: this is how macOS words it for apps that aren't registered with Apple. Click Cancel (not Move to Trash). Open Terminal (Applications › Utilities › Terminal), paste this line, press Return, then open Drift again:

xattr -cr /Applications/Drift.app

It only removes the “downloaded from the internet” mark from Drift. You need to do this once.

On Windows

On Windows

  1. Open the installer you downloaded (Drift-Setup). Drift installs and starts by itself.
  2. If a blue window says Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. It appears because early-access versions aren't registered with Microsoft yet.
  3. Drift lives in the system tray next to the clock. If you don't see its icon, click the small arrow there.

That's it: from now on Drift updates itself, and registered versions will arrive the same way, without the warnings.
